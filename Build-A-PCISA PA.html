The PC Parts Hall of Fame

Every part has a job. Yes, even the box it all goes in.

🧠

CPU — The Brain

Makes the decisions and crunches instructions. Basically the teammate who actually reads the mission briefing.

Brain power
🕸️

Motherboard — The Meetup Spot

Connects the components so they can work together. The group chat of your PC, but with fewer arguments.

Team connector
⚡

RAM — The Short-Term Memory

Keeps active apps and game data ready to use. More room means your PC can juggle more tasks at once.

Multitasking
🎮

GPU — The Artist

Creates the images you see in games and creative apps. Responsible for those glorious graphics settings.

Pixels and frames
📦

Storage — The Treasure Chest

Keeps your games, photos, projects, and files even after shutdown. Your digital hoarding has a home.

Save everything
🔌

PSU — The Power Dealer

Supplies power to the system. Pick one that can handle the parts, or your dream build may not get far.

Power up
🧊

Cooler — The Chill Manager

Helps keep the CPU from overheating. Even computer brains need to cool off under pressure.

Stay frosty
🏠

Case — The Home Base

Houses and protects the parts. It needs enough space for everything to fit comfortably.

Room to fit
🌬️

Case Fans — The Breeze Crew

Move air around the case to help carry heat away. Tiny wind machines, doing important work.

Airflow
